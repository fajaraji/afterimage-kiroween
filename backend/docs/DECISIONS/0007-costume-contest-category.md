# ADR-0007: Costume Contest is the hackathon category

**Status:** accepted (user decision). **Supersedes:** ADR-0005 (Frankenstein category) and the Strands/Bedrock scope in ADR-0004 and ADR-0006.

## Decision
Submit Afterimage in the Kiroween **Costume Contest** category. The product is the teammate's haunted case file: an erased customer returns from a retained backup, and a fresh read proves it. The backend supplies the real AWS evidence behind that story.

## Consequences
- Active specs: 01, 02, 03, 05, 06. Specs 04, 07, 08, 09 are superseded.
- No Strands, Bedrock, Step Functions or SQS work. No agent-action work.
- Frankenstein documents stay as history and are marked superseded.
- If the user reopens the category, start from ADR-0005 and specs 07 and 08 and re-plan; do not assume they still fit.
